# Live Enterprise Decision Intelligence & Predictive AI Engine

An advanced, non-intrusive enterprise intelligence platform designed to seamlessly connect with corporate Excel workflows in real-time. It operates quietly in the background while employees conduct daily operations, running advanced predictive models and risk assessments exclusively for the CEO and executive leadership team.

## Key Architecture & Features
- **Silent Background Processing**: Monitors live data entries from corporate Excel sheets without interrupting employee workflows.
- **High-Accuracy Predictive Engine**: Delivers future trends, revenue forecasts, and operational projections with up to a **90% confidence threshold**.
- **Automated Risk & Anomaly Detection**: Instantly spots supply chain bottlenecks, profit margin erosion, and operational anomalies.
- **Strict Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)**: Ensures analytical dashboards, future forecasts, and mitigation strategies are accessible **exclusively by the CEO and executive board**.
- **English-Native Analytics Workflow**: Built with international corporate standards in mind.

## Core Modules
1. **Excel Stream Connector**: Tracks worksheet mutations and updates asynchronously.
2. **Data Cleansing & Normalization**: Cleans incoming live data streams using Pandas.
3. **Forecasting & ML Core**: Evaluates time-series data to predict business outcomes.
4. **Executive Dashboard API**: Generates secure reports for top management.
